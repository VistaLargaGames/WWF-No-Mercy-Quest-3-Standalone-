WWF-No-Mercy-Quest-3-Standalone-
A standalone port of the WWF No Mercy N64 Recomp that runs natively on the MetaQuest 3.
Take the ring just like we did in the 90's but in VR!

FEATURES
-60FPS
-Immersive view
-PLUG AND PLAY-
Download APK from here and install using your MetaQuest 3 web browser or use sidequest.
Simply provide your copy of the 1.0 North American version of the game in the main menu and start playing! This project will automatically load assets from the provided copy, so there is no need to go through a separate extraction step or build the game yourself.


-KNOWN BUGS-

-random screen stretch/camera snapping here and there.

CONTROLS:

• Left stick moves your wrestler.
• Right stick is the taunt stick.
• A is grapple.
• B is strike.
• X switches who you are focused on.
• Y is the old left-grip action (flip a downed wrestler, grab a weapon).
• Left trigger is block.
• Right trigger steps in or out of the ring. It needs a full pull.
• Left grip is evade, and it is also the pin when you are at their head.
• Right grip is run.
• Click either stick switches that wrestler between you and the CPU.
• Left menu button pauses.
• Meta button re-centers your view.
